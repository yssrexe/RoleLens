================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — LEGAL / ADVOCACY
================================================================================

Job Title   : Legal Advocate / Attorney
Department  : Legal Affairs
Reports To  : Senior Partner / General Counsel
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a skilled and dedicated Legal Advocate to represent clients,
provide sound legal counsel, and navigate complex legal proceedings with
integrity and professionalism.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Advise clients on their legal rights, obligations, and available courses of action.
• Draft, review, and negotiate contracts, agreements, and legal documents.
• Represent clients in court hearings, arbitrations, and mediations.
• Conduct thorough legal research and prepare case strategies.
• File motions, pleadings, and briefs with appropriate courts.
• Maintain confidential client files and case documentation.
• Stay current with changes in legislation and case law.
• Collaborate with paralegals and junior attorneys on complex cases.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Juris Doctor (JD) degree from an accredited law school.
• Admission to the state/national bar association.
• 3+ years of litigation or corporate law experience.
• Strong research, writing, and oral advocacy skills.
• High ethical standards and client-focused approach.
• Experience with legal management software (Clio, MyCase) is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and billable-hour bonuses.
• Partnership track for high performers.
• Continuing legal education (CLE) support.
• Professional liability insurance coverage.
