                    JOB DESCRIPTION — BANKING

Job Title   : Relationship Manager – Retail Banking
Department  : Retail & Personal Banking
Reports To  : Branch Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a client-focused Relationship Manager to build and maintain
strong banking relationships, identify customer financial needs, and deliver
tailored banking solutions that drive satisfaction and portfolio growth.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Develop and manage a portfolio of retail and high-net-worth banking clients.
• Proactively identify and cross-sell banking products (loans, credit cards,
  investments, insurance).
• Conduct financial needs assessments and recommend appropriate solutions.
• Onboard new clients and ensure seamless KYC and account opening processes.
• Achieve branch sales targets for deposits, lending, and fee income.
• Resolve client issues and complaints promptly and professionally.
• Stay informed on regulatory requirements and compliance standards (AML, KYC).
• Prepare client reports and portfolio performance reviews.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Finance, Banking, Business, or related field.
• 2+ years of retail or commercial banking experience.
• Strong knowledge of banking products, credit assessment, and financial planning.
• Excellent interpersonal and sales skills.
• Proficiency with core banking systems (Temenos, Finacle, or similar).
• CFA, CFP, or banking diploma is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive base salary + commission on targets.
• Health and pension benefits.
• Continuous learning and banking certification support.
• Fast-track career growth within the banking group.
