================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — AGRICULTURE
================================================================================

Job Title   : Agricultural Field Manager
Department  : Farm Operations
Reports To  : Director of Agriculture
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site / Field-Based

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking an experienced Agricultural Field Manager to oversee daily farm
operations, manage crop production cycles, and implement sustainable farming
practices that maximize yield and profitability.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Plan and manage planting, irrigation, fertilization, and harvest schedules.
• Monitor crop health and implement pest, disease, and weed control programs.
• Supervise and train farm laborers and seasonal workers.
• Maintain farm equipment and coordinate maintenance and repairs.
• Track operational budgets, input costs, and production yields.
• Implement sustainable and environmentally compliant farming practices.
• Maintain records for regulatory compliance and certifications (organic, GAP).
• Collaborate with agronomists and supply chain teams on market readiness.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Agriculture, Agronomy, or related field.
• 3+ years of farm management or field operations experience.
• Knowledge of crop science, soil management, and irrigation systems.
• Familiarity with agricultural technology (GPS tractors, drone scouting, farm ERP).
• Strong leadership, problem-solving, and organizational skills.
• Valid driver's license; heavy equipment operation experience preferred.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and seasonal performance bonuses.
• On-site housing option (farm-based roles).
• Equipment and tools provided.
• Opportunities to lead large-scale agri-tech initiatives.
