================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — DIGITAL MEDIA
================================================================================

Job Title   : Digital Media Strategist
Department  : Marketing & Digital Media
Reports To  : Head of Digital Marketing
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a creative and data-savvy Digital Media Strategist to plan,
execute, and optimize digital media campaigns across all online channels,
delivering measurable results and brand growth.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Develop integrated digital media strategies across SEO, SEM, social, display,
  and email channels.
• Plan and manage paid media budgets across Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok.
• Create and oversee content calendars aligned with marketing objectives.
• Analyze campaign performance and deliver actionable insights and reports.
• Collaborate with creative teams to produce compelling media assets.
• Conduct A/B testing on ad creatives, landing pages, and audience segments.
• Monitor trends in digital media, algorithms, and emerging platforms.
• Manage relationships with media agencies and platform representatives.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or Media Studies.
• 3+ years in digital media planning or performance marketing.
• Proficiency with Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and DSPs.
• Strong analytical skills and experience with attribution modeling.
• Excellent project management and communication skills.
• Google Ads and Meta Blueprint certifications preferred.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and performance bonuses.
• Hybrid and flexible work arrangement.
• Access to premium marketing tools and platforms.
• High-visibility role with direct impact on brand growth.
