================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — CULINARY / CHEF
================================================================================

Job Title   : Head Chef / Executive Chef
Department  : Food & Beverage – Kitchen Operations
Reports To  : Restaurant General Manager / F&B Director
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a passionate and innovative Executive Chef to lead kitchen
operations, create seasonal menus, and deliver an exceptional dining experience
that reflects our brand's culinary vision and quality standards.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Design, develop, and continuously refine seasonal and special event menus.
• Lead, train, and motivate a brigade of kitchen staff.
• Maintain the highest standards of food quality, presentation, and consistency.
• Oversee food cost management, inventory control, and supplier negotiations.
• Ensure full compliance with food safety and hygiene regulations (HACCP).
• Manage kitchen scheduling, labor budgets, and operational workflows.
• Collaborate with the front-of-house team to deliver seamless dining service.
• Stay current with culinary trends and introduce creative new dishes.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Culinary degree from an accredited institution (preferred).
• 5+ years of progressive kitchen experience with 2+ years in a Head Chef role.
• Deep knowledge of culinary techniques, global cuisines, and flavor profiling.
• Strong leadership, organizational, and time management skills.
• Food Safety Manager Certification (ServSafe or equivalent).
• Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced kitchen environment.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and performance bonuses.
• Creative freedom over menu development.
• Staff meals and culinary training opportunities.
• Pathway to culinary director or multi-venue leadership.
