================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — ACCOUNTING
================================================================================

Job Title   : Senior Accountant
Department  : Finance & Accounting
Reports To  : Finance Manager / CFO
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site / Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a detail-oriented and experienced Senior Accountant to manage
financial records, ensure regulatory compliance, and provide accurate reporting
that supports strategic business decisions.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Prepare and maintain accurate financial statements (income statement,
  balance sheet, cash flow).
• Manage month-end and year-end close processes.
• Reconcile bank statements, accounts payable, and accounts receivable.
• Ensure compliance with GAAP / IFRS standards and local tax regulations.
• Coordinate with external auditors during annual audits.
• Prepare and file tax returns (corporate, VAT, payroll).
• Develop and improve internal accounting controls and procedures.
• Analyze financial data and provide variance reports to management.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field.
• CPA, ACCA, or CMA certification preferred.
• 3+ years of professional accounting experience.
• Proficiency in accounting software (QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle, Xero).
• Strong knowledge of tax laws and financial regulations.
• Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and annual performance bonus.
• Health and retirement benefits.
• Hybrid work flexibility.
• Clear promotion path to Finance Manager.
