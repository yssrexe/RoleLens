                    JOB DESCRIPTION — SALES

Job Title   : Account Executive – B2B Sales
Department  : Sales
Reports To  : Sales Manager / VP of Sales
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid / Remote

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a high-energy and results-driven Account Executive to drive
new business acquisition, manage the full sales cycle, and build lasting client
relationships that contribute to our ambitious revenue growth targets.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Prospect and qualify new B2B leads through outbound and inbound channels.
• Conduct discovery calls, product demos, and solution presentations.
• Develop customized proposals and negotiate contracts to close deals.
• Manage a pipeline of opportunities and accurately forecast revenue in CRM.
• Collaborate with marketing on lead generation and campaign alignment.
• Onboard new clients and ensure a smooth handover to the customer success team.
• Meet and exceed monthly and quarterly sales quotas.
• Attend industry events, trade shows, and networking sessions to build pipeline.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field.
• 3+ years of B2B sales experience with a proven track record of quota attainment.
• Strong prospecting, negotiation, and closing skills.
• Proficiency with CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot).
• Excellent communication and presentation skills.
• Self-motivated with a competitive mindset and resilience under pressure.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive base salary + uncapped commission.
• Accelerators and bonuses for exceeding quota.
• Full benefits: health, dental, vision, and 401(k).
• Fast career progression to Senior AE or Sales Manager.
