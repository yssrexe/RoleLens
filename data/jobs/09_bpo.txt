================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — BPO (BUSINESS PROCESS OUTSOURCING)
================================================================================

Job Title   : BPO Operations Team Leader
Department  : Customer Operations
Reports To  : Operations Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site | Shift-Based

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a proactive and results-driven BPO Team Leader to supervise a
team of customer service agents, ensure KPI targets are met, and drive
continuous improvement in service quality and operational efficiency.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Supervise and coach a team of 12–20 BPO agents across voice and non-voice channels.
• Monitor daily performance against KPIs: AHT, CSAT, FCR, shrinkage, and adherence.
• Conduct regular team huddles, one-on-ones, and quality coaching sessions.
• Handle escalated customer complaints and complex cases.
• Generate and present daily, weekly, and monthly performance reports.
• Collaborate with training teams to identify skill gaps and learning needs.
• Support recruitment and onboarding of new team members.
• Implement process improvements to enhance efficiency and customer experience.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, or related field.
• 2+ years of BPO or call center experience with at least 1 year in a
  supervisory role.
• Strong understanding of contact center metrics and workforce management.
• Excellent communication, leadership, and conflict resolution skills.
• Proficiency with CRM tools (Salesforce, Zendesk) and reporting dashboards.
• Ability to work flexible shifts including nights and weekends.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and shift differentials.
• Performance-based incentives.
• Health and wellness benefits.
• Career advancement into Operations Manager roles.
