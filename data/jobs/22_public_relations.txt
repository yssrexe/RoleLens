================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — PUBLIC RELATIONS
================================================================================

Job Title   : Public Relations Manager
Department  : Communications & PR
Reports To  : Director of Communications / CMO
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a strategic and articulate PR Manager to shape and protect
our brand reputation, build media relationships, and craft compelling narratives
that resonate with diverse audiences across traditional and digital channels.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Develop and execute integrated PR and communications strategies.
• Build and maintain relationships with journalists, editors, and media outlets.
• Write and distribute press releases, media pitches, op-eds, and talking points.
• Manage media inquiries and serve as a company spokesperson when required.
• Monitor media coverage and report on brand sentiment and PR KPIs.
• Coordinate press events, product launches, media briefings, and interviews.
• Handle crisis communications with speed, accuracy, and professionalism.
• Collaborate with marketing, social media, and executive teams on messaging.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Public Relations, Communications, Journalism, or related field.
• 4+ years of PR experience (agency or in-house).
• Established media contacts across national and trade publications.
• Exceptional writing, editing, and verbal communication skills.
• Experience with PR tools (Muck Rack, Cision, PR Newswire).
• Ability to manage multiple campaigns and deadlines simultaneously.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and performance incentives.
• High-profile media exposure and executive collaboration.
• Hybrid work flexibility.
• Professional development through industry PR events and training.
