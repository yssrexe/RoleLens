                    JOB DESCRIPTION — ENGINEERING
================================================================================

Job Title   : Mechanical Engineer – Product Development
Department  : Engineering & R&D
Reports To  : Chief Engineer / Engineering Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site / Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for an innovative Mechanical Engineer to design, develop, and
test mechanical systems and components, bringing new products from concept to
production with efficiency and precision.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Design mechanical components and assemblies using CAD software (SolidWorks,
  AutoCAD, CATIA).
• Perform engineering calculations, simulations (FEA/CFD), and tolerance analyses.
• Develop prototypes and coordinate with manufacturing for design validation.
• Write test plans, conduct experiments, and analyze results to validate designs.
• Manage engineering change orders (ECOs) and maintain technical documentation.
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams (electrical, software, procurement).
• Ensure designs comply with industry standards (ASME, ISO, CE, UL).
• Identify and resolve design issues through root cause analysis.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering (Master's preferred).
• 3+ years of product design or manufacturing engineering experience.
• Proficiency in SolidWorks, AutoCAD, or equivalent CAD tools.
• Experience with FEA/CFD analysis (ANSYS, COMSOL).
• Strong understanding of GD&T, materials science, and manufacturing processes.
• Excellent problem-solving skills and engineering judgment.
• PE license is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and innovation bonuses.
• Access to state-of-the-art engineering labs and tools.
• Sponsorship for PE licensure and professional certifications.
• Collaborative environment working on cutting-edge products.
