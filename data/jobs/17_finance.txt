                    JOB DESCRIPTION — FINANCE
================================================================================

Job Title   : Financial Analyst
Department  : Corporate Finance
Reports To  : Finance Director / CFO
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a sharp and analytical Financial Analyst to support strategic
financial planning, budgeting, and investment analysis that drives informed
decision-making across the organization.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Build and maintain complex financial models for forecasting, valuation,
  and scenario analysis.
• Prepare monthly management accounts, budget variance reports, and KPI dashboards.
• Conduct DCF, comparable company analysis, and M&A financial due diligence.
• Support the annual budgeting and rolling forecast processes.
• Analyze business unit performance and recommend cost optimization strategies.
• Prepare board-level financial presentations and investment proposals.
• Monitor cash flow, working capital, and debt covenants.
• Coordinate with accounting, operations, and strategy teams on financial matters.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, or Accounting.
• CFA candidate or charterholder preferred.
• 2–4 years of financial analysis, investment banking, or FP&A experience.
• Advanced Excel and financial modeling skills.
• Proficiency with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle) and BI tools (Power BI, Tableau).
• Strong communication skills for presenting financial data to non-finance audiences.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and performance bonus.
• CFA exam sponsorship and study support.
• Exposure to senior leadership and strategic projects.
• Clear path to Senior Analyst or Finance Manager.
