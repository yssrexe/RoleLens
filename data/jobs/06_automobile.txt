================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — AUTOMOBILE
================================================================================

Job Title   : Automotive Service Technician
Department  : Service & Maintenance
Reports To  : Service Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a skilled and certified Automotive Service Technician to
diagnose, repair, and maintain vehicles to the highest safety and quality
standards, delivering an outstanding experience to every customer.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Perform diagnostic tests on vehicle systems (engine, transmission, brakes,
  electrical, HVAC).
• Execute routine maintenance: oil changes, tire rotations, fluid flushes,
  filter replacements.
• Repair or replace defective parts using manufacturer-approved procedures.
• Use diagnostic software (OBD-II, dealer-specific tools) to identify fault codes.
• Document all work performed in the dealership management system.
• Communicate repair findings and recommendations clearly to service advisors.
• Maintain a clean and organized workstation.
• Stay current with OEM technical service bulletins (TSBs) and recalls.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• High school diploma or equivalent; vocational/technical certification preferred.
• ASE certification (A1–A8) required; Master Technician preferred.
• 3+ years of automotive repair experience.
• Proficiency with hand tools, power tools, and diagnostic equipment.
• Valid driver's license with a clean driving record.
• Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive flat-rate pay or hourly wage.
• Tool allowance and uniform provided.
• Manufacturer training and ASE certification reimbursement.
• Health insurance and retirement benefits.
