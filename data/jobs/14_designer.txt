                    JOB DESCRIPTION — DESIGN
================================================================================

Job Title   : Senior UX/UI Designer
Department  : Product Design
Reports To  : Head of Design / CPO
Employment  : Full-Time | Remote-Friendly

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a talented Senior UX/UI Designer to craft intuitive, beautiful,
and user-centered digital experiences that delight users and drive business goals
across web and mobile platforms.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Lead UX research: user interviews, surveys, usability testing, and journey mapping.
• Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups for web and mobile.
• Define and maintain the design system and component library.
• Collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders.
• Present design concepts and rationale to cross-functional teams.
• Iterate rapidly on designs based on user feedback and data insights.
• Ensure accessibility (WCAG 2.1) and inclusive design across all deliverables.
• Mentor junior designers and uphold design quality standards.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Interaction Design, Graphic Design, or HCI.
• 4+ years of UX/UI design experience (product or agency).
• Expert proficiency in Figma; experience with Sketch or Adobe XD.
• Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process.
• Solid understanding of front-end constraints (HTML, CSS basics).
• Data-driven design approach with experience in A/B testing.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and design tool subscriptions.
• Remote-first culture with flexible hours.
• Impactful role shaping products used by millions.
• Annual design conference and learning budget.
