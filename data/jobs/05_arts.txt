================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — ARTS
================================================================================

Job Title   : Visual Artist & Creative Content Specialist
Department  : Creative Arts
Reports To  : Art Director
Employment  : Full-Time | Studio-Based / Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a talented and versatile Visual Artist to produce original artwork,
visual concepts, and creative content that strengthens our brand identity and
engages diverse audiences across print and digital media.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Create original illustrations, paintings, graphic compositions, and mixed-media work.
• Develop visual concepts for campaigns, exhibitions, and branded content.
• Collaborate with marketing, editorial, and design teams on creative projects.
• Produce art for digital platforms, merchandise, and print publications.
• Maintain and manage a portfolio of completed and in-progress works.
• Stay informed on contemporary art movements, techniques, and cultural trends.
• Participate in exhibitions, art fairs, and community creative events.
• Mentor junior artists and interns within the creative department.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts, Illustration, or Graphic Arts.
• 2+ years of professional artistic or creative content experience.
• Strong portfolio demonstrating range of style and medium.
• Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign).
• Ability to work to briefs while maintaining creative originality.
• Strong communication and time management skills.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and art supply stipend.
• Studio workspace and exhibition opportunities.
• Creative autonomy and collaborative environment.
• Annual participation in international art events.
