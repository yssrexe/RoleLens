================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — CONSTRUCTION
================================================================================

Job Title   : Construction Project Manager
Department  : Project Management
Reports To  : Director of Construction / VP of Operations
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site / Field-Based

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for an experienced Construction Project Manager to oversee
all phases of construction projects from pre-construction planning through
final delivery, ensuring projects are completed on time, within budget,
and to the highest quality standards.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Manage all aspects of construction projects: scope, schedule, budget, and quality.
• Coordinate with architects, engineers, subcontractors, and suppliers.
• Develop and maintain detailed project schedules using MS Project or Primavera.
• Monitor and control project costs; prepare budget forecasts and variance reports.
• Lead daily site meetings and weekly progress reviews with stakeholders.
• Ensure compliance with local building codes, safety regulations, and permits.
• Manage RFIs, submittals, change orders, and contract documentation.
• Resolve on-site issues and make timely decisions to keep projects on track.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Management, or related field.
• 5+ years of project management experience in residential or commercial construction.
• PMP or CCM certification preferred.
• Proficiency with Procore, MS Project, AutoCAD, or Primavera P6.
• Strong knowledge of construction contracts (AIA, FIDIC).
• Excellent leadership, negotiation, and problem-solving skills.
• OSHA 30-Hour certification preferred.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary + project completion bonuses.
• Company vehicle or travel allowance.
• Health and retirement benefits.
• Opportunity to lead landmark construction projects.
