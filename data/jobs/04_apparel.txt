================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — APPAREL
================================================================================

Job Title   : Apparel Product Developer
Department  : Design & Product Development
Reports To  : Head of Product / Creative Director
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site / Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a creative and technically skilled Apparel Product Developer
to bring clothing collections from concept to production, ensuring quality,
cost-efficiency, and market alignment at every stage.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Collaborate with designers to translate sketches into technical specifications.
• Develop tech packs including measurements, materials, trims, and construction details.
• Source fabrics, trims, and manufacturers domestically and internationally.
• Manage sample development, fit sessions, and revision cycles.
• Negotiate pricing and lead times with vendors and factories.
• Ensure products meet quality, compliance, and safety standards.
• Track development calendars and ensure on-time delivery.
• Analyze market trends and consumer feedback to inform future collections.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Fashion Design, Textile Science, or related field.
• 3+ years of apparel product development or technical design experience.
• Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, CLO 3D, or similar tools.
• Strong understanding of garment construction, fabrics, and manufacturing.
• Excellent communication skills for working with global suppliers.
• Detail-oriented with strong project management abilities.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and clothing allowance.
• Creative and fast-paced team environment.
• International travel opportunities (trade shows, factory visits).
• Employee discounts on all product lines.
