================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — AVIATION
================================================================================

Job Title   : Commercial Airline Pilot (First Officer)
Department  : Flight Operations
Reports To  : Chief Pilot / Director of Operations
Employment  : Full-Time | Rotational Schedule

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a qualified and safety-focused First Officer to join our
commercial flight operations team, operating scheduled passenger services
with professionalism and adherence to all aviation regulations.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Conduct pre-flight inspections, weather briefings, and flight plan reviews.
• Operate aircraft in collaboration with the Captain during all phases of flight.
• Monitor aircraft systems, fuel consumption, and flight parameters.
• Communicate with ATC and ground crews for safe and efficient operations.
• Execute emergency and abnormal procedures per SOPs.
• Complete accurate post-flight documentation and reports.
• Participate in recurrent simulator training and proficiency checks.
• Uphold strict adherence to aviation authority regulations (FAA / EASA / GCAA).

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Airline Transport Pilot License (ATPL) or CPL with ATPL theory credits.
• Minimum 1,500 flight hours total time (per jurisdiction requirements).
• Valid Class 1 Medical Certificate.
• Type rating on B737, A320, or equivalent narrow-body aircraft preferred.
• Strong CRM, situational awareness, and decision-making skills.
• Fluency in English (aviation standard).

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary with flight-hour pay and layover allowances.
• Full benefits: health, life insurance, and retirement plan.
• Type rating sponsorship for the right candidate.
• Structured upgrade path to Captain.
