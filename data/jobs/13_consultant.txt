================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — CONSULTING
================================================================================

Job Title   : Management Consultant
Department  : Strategy & Advisory
Reports To  : Engagement Manager / Principal Consultant
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid / Client-Site Travel Required

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking an analytical and client-focused Management Consultant to help
organizations solve complex business challenges, improve performance, and
implement strategic transformations.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Conduct in-depth analysis of client operations, processes, and market positions.
• Develop strategic recommendations backed by data, research, and benchmarking.
• Design and facilitate workshops, interviews, and focus groups with client teams.
• Build and present high-quality reports, slide decks, and business cases to
  C-suite stakeholders.
• Lead workstreams and coordinate junior consultants on engagements.
• Monitor implementation of recommendations and measure business impact.
• Build long-term client relationships and identify opportunities for follow-on work.
• Stay current with industry trends, frameworks, and best practices.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree required; MBA or Master's from a top institution preferred.
• 3+ years of management consulting or corporate strategy experience.
• Strong analytical and quantitative skills; proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint.
• Exceptional written and verbal communication skills.
• Ability to manage ambiguity and work effectively under tight deadlines.
• Experience in at least one industry vertical (finance, healthcare, tech, retail).

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary + performance bonus.
• Travel allowances and premium client exposure.
• Fast-track career progression.
• Access to world-class training and thought leadership.
