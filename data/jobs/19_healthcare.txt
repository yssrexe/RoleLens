                    JOB DESCRIPTION — HEALTHCARE
================================================================================

Job Title   : Registered Nurse (RN) – Medical/Surgical Unit
Department  : Nursing
Reports To  : Head Nurse / Nursing Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | Shift-Based

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a compassionate and skilled Registered Nurse to join our
Medical/Surgical Unit. You will deliver evidence-based patient care, collaborate
with interdisciplinary teams, and uphold patient dignity and safety at all times.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Assess, plan, implement, and evaluate individualized patient care plans.
• Administer medications and treatments per physician orders.
• Monitor and document vital signs, symptoms, and patient progress.
• Educate patients and families on diagnoses, care plans, and discharge instructions.
• Coordinate with physicians, pharmacists, and allied health staff.
• Respond promptly to emergencies and changing patient conditions.
• Maintain accurate medical records in compliance with HIPAA regulations.
• Mentor junior nursing staff and student nurses as needed.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) required; MSN preferred.
• Valid RN license in the state of practice.
• BLS and ACLS certifications (current).
• Minimum 2 years of clinical nursing experience.
• Proficiency with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems.
• Strong critical thinking, communication, and empathy skills.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary + shift differentials.
• Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance.
• Continuing education reimbursement.
• Paid time off and employee wellness programs.
