                    JOB DESCRIPTION — INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

Job Title   : Senior Software Engineer – Backend
Department  : Engineering / IT
Reports To  : Engineering Manager / CTO
Employment  : Full-Time | Remote / Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer to design and build
scalable backend systems, APIs, and microservices that power our core products
and serve millions of users globally.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Architect, develop, and maintain high-performance backend services and REST/GraphQL APIs.
• Write clean, testable, and well-documented code following best practices.
• Participate in system design discussions and technical architecture reviews.
• Lead code reviews and mentor junior and mid-level engineers.
• Optimize application performance, reliability, and scalability.
• Collaborate with front-end engineers, DevOps, and product managers.
• Identify and resolve production incidents and technical debt.
• Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and deployment processes.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
• 5+ years of backend software development experience.
• Proficiency in Python, Go, Java, or Node.js.
• Strong experience with databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis).
• Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization (Docker, K8s).
• Solid understanding of distributed systems, REST APIs, and microservices.
• Experience with Agile/Scrum development methodologies.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary + equity package.
• Fully remote or hybrid work flexibility.
• Learning budget and conference attendance.
• Work on products with global-scale impact.
