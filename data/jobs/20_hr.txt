                    JOB DESCRIPTION — HUMAN RESOURCES
================================================================================

Job Title   : HR Business Partner (HRBP)
Department  : Human Resources
Reports To  : VP of Human Resources
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are hiring a strategic HR Business Partner to act as a trusted advisor to
department leaders, aligning people strategies with business objectives across
the organization.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Partner with business unit leaders on workforce planning, talent management,
  and organizational design.
• Lead employee relations matters including conflict resolution, investigations,
  and performance improvement plans.
• Drive talent acquisition efforts in coordination with the recruiting team.
• Implement HR policies and ensure consistent application across departments.
• Analyze HR metrics (turnover, engagement, headcount) and recommend actions.
• Support learning & development programs and succession planning.
• Ensure compliance with local labor laws and company policies.
• Champion diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field.
• 4+ years of HR generalist or HRBP experience.
• SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP or PHR / SPHR certification preferred.
• Strong knowledge of employment law and HR best practices.
• Excellent interpersonal, coaching, and conflict resolution skills.
• Proficiency with HRIS platforms (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, BambooHR).

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary and performance bonus.
• Flexible hybrid work arrangement.
• Professional development and certification support.
• Inclusive and people-first culture.
