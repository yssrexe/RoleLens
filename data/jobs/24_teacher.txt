                    JOB DESCRIPTION — EDUCATION / TEACHING
================================================================================

Job Title   : Secondary School Mathematics Teacher
Department  : Academic – STEM
Reports To  : Head of Mathematics / Academic Principal
Employment  : Full-Time | Academic Year Contract

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are seeking a passionate and qualified Mathematics Teacher to inspire
secondary school students (Grades 7–12) through engaging, student-centered
instruction that builds critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Plan, prepare, and deliver high-quality mathematics lessons aligned to
  the national curriculum.
• Differentiate instruction to meet diverse learning needs and abilities.
• Assess student progress through formative and summative evaluations.
• Provide timely, constructive feedback to students and parents.
• Maintain accurate attendance records and grade books.
• Participate in parent-teacher conferences, staff meetings, and professional
  development sessions.
• Integrate technology and real-world applications into lesson design.
• Foster a positive, respectful, and inclusive classroom environment.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Mathematics or Mathematics Education (required).
• State/national teaching certification or license.
• 1+ year of classroom teaching experience (student teaching accepted).
• Strong command of algebra, geometry, calculus, and statistics.
• Excellent communication, patience, and classroom management skills.
• Experience with LMS platforms (Google Classroom, Canvas) is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary on the official teachers' pay scale.
• Health insurance and pension/retirement contributions.
• Annual professional development budget.
• Supportive academic community and mentoring program.
