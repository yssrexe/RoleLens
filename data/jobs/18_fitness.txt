================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — FITNESS
================================================================================

Job Title   : Certified Personal Trainer & Fitness Coach
Department  : Health & Wellness
Reports To  : Fitness Director / Gym Manager
Employment  : Full-Time | On-site

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a passionate and certified Personal Trainer to design
individualized fitness programs, motivate clients to reach their health goals,
and create an energetic and supportive gym environment.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Conduct initial fitness assessments and health history consultations.
• Design and deliver personalized training programs aligned to client goals
  (weight loss, muscle gain, athletic performance, rehabilitation).
• Lead individual and group training sessions with energy and expertise.
• Track and record client progress, adjusting programs as needed.
• Educate clients on nutrition fundamentals, recovery, and healthy habits.
• Ensure safe use of gym equipment and correct exercise technique.
• Promote and sell personal training packages to prospective members.
• Maintain a clean, safe, and welcoming training environment.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Certified Personal Trainer (NASM-CPT, ACE, ISSA, or equivalent).
• CPR / AED certification (current).
• 2+ years of personal training or group fitness experience.
• Strong knowledge of exercise physiology, anatomy, and nutrition basics.
• Motivating personality with excellent client communication skills.
• Experience with special populations (seniors, post-rehab) is a plus.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Base salary + commission on personal training packages sold.
• Free gym membership and fitness perks.
• Continuing education and re-certification support.
• Flexible scheduling and a health-focused work culture.
