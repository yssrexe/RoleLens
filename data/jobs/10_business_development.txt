================================================================================
                    JOB DESCRIPTION — BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT
================================================================================

Job Title   : Business Development Manager
Department  : Sales & Business Development
Reports To  : VP of Business Development
Employment  : Full-Time | Hybrid

ABOUT THE ROLE
--------------
We are looking for a driven and strategic Business Development Manager to
identify new market opportunities, build high-value partnerships, and lead
initiatives that accelerate company revenue and market presence.

RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------
• Identify and qualify new business opportunities through research, networking,
  and outbound prospecting.
• Build and nurture relationships with potential clients, partners, and investors.
• Develop and deliver compelling proposals, pitches, and presentations.
• Negotiate and close partnership agreements and commercial contracts.
• Collaborate with marketing, product, and sales teams on go-to-market strategies.
• Analyze market trends, competitor activity, and customer insights.
• Track pipeline activity and revenue forecasts using CRM tools.
• Represent the company at industry events, conferences, and trade shows.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field; MBA preferred.
• 4+ years of business development, sales, or strategic partnerships experience.
• Proven track record of meeting or exceeding revenue targets.
• Excellent negotiation, communication, and presentation skills.
• Proficiency with CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot).
• Strong analytical and strategic thinking capabilities.

WHAT WE OFFER
-------------
• Competitive salary + uncapped commission structure.
• Equity / profit-sharing options for senior hires.
• Flexible hybrid work environment.
• High-impact role with direct influence on company growth.
